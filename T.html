Tribute to Monsignor Agustín Román

This is a simple tribute to our very dear Father and spiritual guide Monsignor Agustín Román, who recently passed away. In 2008 we presented him, together with the esteemed journalist Ariel Remos, of the 2008 Heritage Award. The original page also preserves images from the event and a video recording.
