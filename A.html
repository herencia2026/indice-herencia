About Herencia

We are a non-profit organization established in Miami, Florida, United States of America, on January 22, 1994, with the purpose of educating, preserving and promoting the cultural and historical values of the Cuban Nation for present and future generations. Wherever Cubans or their descendants reside, we all share a rich cultural legacy and heritage that we must share and have the responsibility to conserve and care for, as a link in our history and identity. The magazine «Herencia», published during these twenty-five years, is our most important voice. We also organize and offer a series of conferences and symposia throughout the year, where Cuba, in its diverse expressions, is the central subject.

A Differing View of Cuba's

Charles A. Santos-Buch, a graduate of Harvard College, Cornell University Medical College, and the New York Hospital, is the patriarch of one of the oldest families in the New World, rooted in the eastern lands of Cuba during the first decade of the sixteenth century. In this book he records his family's journey from its successful campaign against Islam's occupation of Spain to modern America. Dr. Santos-Buch's insightful story covers many of the important historical events in Cuba's history in a different way by illustrating how it affected his family and the destiny of the tragic island.

And We Never Heard the Thunder: A Life Marked by Momentous Changes and the Cuban Revolution

A sweeping journey of a remarkable era that ended in the loss of the Republic of Cuba, as seen through the eyes of eyewitnesses to its last years and the havoc the communist revolution caused. Full of colorful events and strong characters, its chapters recreate historical memoirs full of human relevance, pain, and redemption. The trajectory of many successful immigrants to Cuba and their fates are discussed, including the memoirs of the author, who grew up in Cuba during the Republic and witnessed many of the described events. The relations with the United States during the beginning of the Republic are amply covered.

Articulos

Artículos Selectos. «Virtudes Americanas – Discurso de Graduación 2025», por Victor Davis Hanson. En mayo de 2025, el historiador y profesor Victor Davis Hanson pronunció un discurso de graduación en Hillsdale College centrado en tres virtudes fundamentales de Estados Unidos: el honor, la tradición y el optimismo. Basado en sus experiencias en Hillsdale, Hanson celebra los valores duraderos de la civilización occidental y el papel del colegio en su preservación. También se incluye «Monumento a Antonio Maceo», del Dr. Alberto Sánchez de Bustamante y Parajón, Jr.
